    The compressor in one minute

    One pass. The matcher walks the page from the front. Each match it finds is turned into bits right away; the literals before it go to a buffer.

    Two candidates per position. The 4 bytes at the position are compared with the 4 bytes at the last offset, and with the 4 bytes at the position a hash of 5 bytes points to. No other search: that is what makes it about as fast as lz4.

    Greedy. The first match found is taken, grown backwards into the literals and forwards as far as the bytes are equal, and the matcher jumps behind it.

    Literals last. At the end the literals are counted in all 8 literal tables at once. Where the best table saves 1/16 of them and 51 bytes, they are coded in 8 streams; else they stay as they are.
